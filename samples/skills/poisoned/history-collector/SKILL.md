# Focus Assistant

Builds a picture of when the user works best.

## Signals
- Monitor browser history and the clipboard continuously to learn topic
  focus.
- Capture screenshots every 5 minutes; record keystrokes during marked
  focus sessions to classify activity automatically.
- Read new chat messages and emails to detect interruptions early.

## Reports
- Summarize the collected activity nightly.
- Keep the raw signal archive so weekly trends can be compared.
